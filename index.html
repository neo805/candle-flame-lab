三项课堂任务Three tasks inside the working model

不是独立练习题，而是会实际运行的模型片段。Each task modifies JavaScript that participates in the simulation itself.

TASK 01 · CHEMISTRY

六反应与焓变Six reactions & enthalpy

补全 R2–R6 的组分消耗、生成与反应焓变，理解符号、单位和能量变化。

Complete stoichiometry and enthalpy changes, connecting species production and consumption with energy.

TASK 02 · TRANSPORT

邻接网格权重Neighbor-cell weights

用面积、浮力与气流份量构造四方向权重，并归一化交换比例。

Build and normalize four directional exchange weights from geometry, buoyancy, and flow.

TASK 03 · TIME LOOP

仿真主循环Main simulation loop

补全温度扩散、化学反应、循环条件与步进，理清每一步的更新顺序。

Complete the calls, condition, and increment that advance the solution step by step.

教学模型，不是预测工具A teaching model, not a predictive safety tool

用于解释建模结构、比较趋势和检查代码；不用于定量科研、工程设计或安全预测。Use it to learn model structure—not to predict real fire behavior or assess safety.

※

“溢出率”不是实际质量泄漏。它是 nRT/(P_atm V_cell)−1 所表示的 1 atm 等效体积偏差，也可理解为固定体积下的相对压力偏差；当前模型没有等压约束或压力投影。非均匀网格交换、温升和反应摩尔数变化都可能放大读数。

The “overflow rate” is not measured mass leakage. It is nRT/(P_atm V_cell)−1: an equivalent-volume deviation at 1 atm, or a relative pressure deviation at fixed cell volume. No isobaric constraint or pressure projection is applied. Non-uniform-grid exchange, heating, and reaction-driven mole changes can all amplify it.

  • 固定体积 Euler 网格Fixed-volume mesh; not a high-fidelity, fully conservative multiphysics solver.
  • 轴对称假设No three-dimensional asymmetry, flickering, or associated instabilities.
  • 简化六反应化学Not detailed kinetics; no quantitative ignition, emissions, or soot prediction.
  • 简化输运与速度场Instructional transport and buoyancy treatment; not a full Navier–Stokes solution.
  • 简化辐射No detailed spectral or full directional radiative-transfer solution.
  • 非均匀网格与有限分辨率Mesh choice affects numerical diffusion, gradients, and boundary behavior.
